# Public aRTF training-only visual teacher v1

Only the 24 original, label-free public aRTF training images T01–T24 may be viewed by the current chat visual assistant. No AI Hub pixels, evaluation photographs, original coordinates, model overlays or per-image scores may be supplied. This is interactive assistant vision, not a separately called revision-pinned API. Freeze the entire response before inspecting publisher coordinates. No claims of human verification or precise cm truth.

Coordinates refer to upright original RGB, top-left origin, normalized x/512,y/256. Return all requested targets with visible_proposal, uncertain_proposal or abstain (null x,y). Unknown seams must remain abstentions. Selection is deterministic by source hash; do not replace difficult photographs.

Tshirt: shoulder_left/right, armpit_left/right, length_start, hem_center, sleeve_start, sleeve_end. Follow aRTF whole-garment sides: left is the side whose lower hem endpoint is nearest the garment bounding-box bottom-left in original pixel distance, even when the shirt is rotated. Shoulder is sleeve-attachment seam, not outer cuff; armpit is underarm junction. Additional provisional measurements follow existing Coordit procedural semantics: length_start is the left high shoulder/neck junction (not neck center), hem_center is the visible lower hem midpoint, sleeve_start is the RIGHT shoulder junction and sleeve_end the midpoint of that right sleeve's cuff. Do not synthesize a hidden hem or neck junction. These additional semantics are development hypotheses, not certified tailoring protocols; they must not be enabled for production or used as evaluation truth.

Shorts: waist_left/right, crotch, side_hem, waist_center. Left waistband endpoint is nearest bbox top-left; side_hem is corresponding pipe_left_outer. Waist_center is the visible top waistband center along the garment centerline, not a folded-image bbox midpoint. Crotch is the visible inseam junction. No ungrounded hip endpoints. Duplicate side_waist is omitted rather than counted as another target.

Frozen AI coordinates are unverified weak targets. Preserve every publisher supervised point, visibility flag and mask. Reject AI targets when the corresponding publisher point is not visible, or differs by more than 0.05 normalized square-input diagonal. Such filtering measures agreement, not independent accuracy. All abstentions are excluded from the weak loss without being recast as invisible labels. Existing-target proposals and new unverified endpoint proposals must be counted separately. Use weak coordinate/heatmap supervision with global weight 0.25, uncertain proposals further multiplied by 0.25. Do not add teacher-based visibility BCE, centimeter targets, masks or category labels. Keep new teacher-only heads unavailable to production inference.

Continue the pinned latest model in a separate output directory. Also run a no-teacher control with exactly the same parent, seed, epochs, optimizer, samples and brightness-only augmentation. Predeclare 12 epochs, 512 exposures/epoch, batch 4, learning rate 0.0001; source fractions AI Hub 0.1 (local masks/classification only), aRTF 0.85, approved body-free GCD 0.05. No geometric augmentation is allowed while weak coordinates are untransformed. Preserve all checkpoints and source manifests. Evaluate only final checkpoints, without tuning on test results, on the frozen 561-photo development evaluation pool, and separately the previous 8-photo pilot subset. Report PCK@0.05, NME, mIoU and projected-pixel segment relative errors; actual AR cm accuracy is unavailable. Do not import evaluation proposals into training. No Phase 3 changes.
