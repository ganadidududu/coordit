Approval is scoped to the recorded uses and original publisher representations, not a legal opinion about unrelated mirrors. Training must fail on unknown sources, missing provenance, denied evaluation groups, or missing rights. Never infer image rights from annotation/code licenses.

- aRTF v1.0.1: original creators' [repository statement](https://github.com/tlpss/aRTF-Clothes-dataset) and [Zenodo deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/14501499), CC BY 4.0. Preserve attribution and describe resizing/conversion. Publisher reports 1,886 total images including towels and incompletely annotated boxers; this is not an accepted training count. All publisher test scenes/garments are excluded from training/validation because of the existing 16-image development set.
- Coordit procedural: generated by repository code using only math, polygons and colors; internal project rights, no external photo/texture/mesh assets. Synthetic accuracy is never real-photo or tape-measure accuracy.
- [Open Images](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/factsfigures_v7.html): Apache annotations, separate image rights; per-file creator/license verification required. No bulk images accepted. Missing seam landmarks remain missing.
- [Fashionpedia](https://fashionpedia.github.io/home/data_license.html): annotations/ontology CC BY 4.0, original image owners retain rights. No image acceptance without per-file clearance.
- Roboflow: all 7 requested candidates inspected, all pending. Original rights and account-specific [platform terms](https://roboflow.com/terms) unverified. 17,000-image candidate uses taxonomy resembling DeepFashion2; this is a risk signal, not proof of origin. Counts are publisher listings, not unique approved images.
- DeepFashion, DeepFashion2, ModaNet: blocked without a separately documented commercial rights grant.

At the earlier Phase 2 checkpoint, additional searches had not established another suitable original-image rights chain; the subsequent original-author Second-Hand Fashion approval is recorded below. [GarmageSet](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Style3D/GarmageSet) is blocked (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). GarmentCodeData was initially pending after an ETH HTTP 403; the subsequent **geometry-only** approval below supersedes that status. Marketplace tags and mirrors are insufficient.

Registry changes require review of publisher authority, image and annotation terms, training/modification/distribution scope, attribution, dates and per-file SHA-256/source evidence. CC BY-SA/ODbL require a separate obligations analysis and remain pending. User captures and corrections are not training data: explicit opt-in, revocation/deletion policy and separately recorded consent are required.

## Seam data integration (2026-10-28)

aRTF direct anatomical candidate mappings are reviewed against the publisher's keypoint definitions and image-based side ordering at revision `e3eda811bca47584f1bb9432e3f11366ccef1e4a`; see `ml/registry/reviews/artf-semantic-mapping-v1.json`. Of 524 existing training-only photos, 210 T-shirts and 104 shorts now expose supported labels. Towels remain mask/classification auxiliaries. Publisher anatomical labels are **not certified tailoring seams or cm truth**. Missing hip, sleeve/length endpoints and waist center are not invented. All publisher test families remain excluded; these photos cannot constitute a new independent evaluation.

[GarmentCodeData v2 official ETH deposit](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/items/9d16a4da-0d30-4963-8842-af20fcf82899) specifies CC BY 4.0. Its official [libdrive distribution](https://libdrive.ethz.ch/index.php/s/4UtC8smtLOGwKoZ) has a `doi.txt` matching `10.3929/ethz-b-000690432`; the actual v2 documentation and official design split were downloaded and hashed. Review `ml/registry/reviews/garmentcodedata-v2-geometry.json` approves **publisher garment sewing patterns/meshes and Coordit body-free, texture-free derivatives**. It excludes raw CAESAR-derived bodies, body measurements, original textures/renders and third-party mirrors. GarmentCode's separate MIT code grant is not used to infer data rights; no generator, Warp or external pretrained weights are bundled.

Only the first 32 MiB of one 5,121,727,032-byte archive was fetched, not the full archive or full dataset. Fifteen complete geometry folders were extracted with per-file SHA-256. Six explicitly selected official-training designs produce 48 new synthetic views; the other nine designs are excluded. UV panel topology supplies unambiguous shoulder/underarm/crotch and some waist junctions; uncertain points are omitted, occluded points remain visibility=1. Draped-body distances are not flat-lay measurement truth. Approval relies on the original publisher's rights representation and mandatory attribution/modification notices; application/server release remains separately blocked.

## Original-author Second-Hand Fashion acquisition (2026-10-27)

[Clothing Dataset for Second-Hand Fashion v3](https://zenodo.org/records/13788681) records original institutional photography at RISE/Wargön Innovation/Myrorna and expert garment-type annotation, CC BY 4.0. The depositor's [own article](https://fnauman.com/second-hand-fashion/posts/2025-02-06-data-exploration/) links his [front-only RGB derivative](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fnauman/fashion-second-hand-front-only-rgb). This author-to-institution-to-repository chain, rather than a mirror's license badge, is the approval basis in `ml/registry/reviews/secondhand-v3-front.json`. Public download requires no account; [Hugging Face terms](https://huggingface.co/terms-of-service) are recorded separately from the [CC BY 4.0 grant](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en). Approval relies on original publisher representations; it is not an independent legal certification of all underlying ownership.

All 14 front-only parquet files at revision `b2559ac0157ea9b6913a65062877f487952e690b` were downloaded and verified against publisher LFS SHA-256/size metadata. The original 26.8 GB Zenodo ZIP was **not** downloaded. The derivative has 31,638 embedded front photographs; 3,390 publisher test rows are excluded. Conversion accepted 14,101 distinct metadata-free normalized photos and excluded 126 exact duplicates plus non-target/ambiguous types. Expert types supervise classification only; 9 coats and 4 sweatshirts have separately recorded weak visual fine labels, not expert fine-category validation. No real mask, semantic seam or cm ground truth was created. Source photos, original pixel hashes, derivative shard pins, source dates and modifications are inventoried. Attribution appears in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; binaries are not redistributed in Git or the app.

The entire accepted pool is training-only: conservative capture-day and perceptual-similarity grouping connects the pool, so an artificial independent split is not claimed. The held-out publisher test was not used for training or visual category curation. Rights approval does not imply suitability for cm evaluation. Exact category counts and rejection reasons are in `ml/reports/DATA_COUNTS.json` and `secondhand-conversion.json`.

Further candidates remain unapproved: [NTNU garment data](https://zenodo.org/records/20440761) declares CC BY-SA 4.0 and needs an obligations review; only metadata was obtained, no images. [Alexey Grigorev's clothing dataset](https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/clothing-dataset) declares CC0 but a complete image-level uploader/consent chain was not established, so no images entered training. These candidates do not increase approved image counts.
