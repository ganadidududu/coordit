# Model rights register

Authority: `ml/registry/MODEL_MANIFEST.json`. App and server artifact lists are separate and currently empty. No third-party weights are bundled or downloaded.

[Depth Anything V2](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2) identifies Small as Apache-2.0 and Base/Large/Giant as CC BY-NC 4.0. The [Metric Hypersim Small card](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Hypersim-Small) says Apache-2.0, but [Hypersim](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-hypersim) dataset uses CC BY-SA 3.0. Asset, share-alike and teacher/pretraining lineage obligations are unresolved: Metric Small remains pending, not deployed.

[MMPose](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmpose) Apache code does not clear individual checkpoints/data. [SAM 2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2) code/checkpoint statements do not establish a garment seam model or complete training-data rights. [RF-DETR](https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr) differentiates Apache-designated components/weights and Plus PML terms. All selected checkpoint candidates remain pending until their exact hash and lineage are reviewed. AGPL/NC weights are blocked by default.

The implemented Coordit baseline is randomly initialized and trains only through the approved loader. CPU smoke and two Metal GPU 20-epoch development runs were executed; none is a production model. Exact hashes and separate empty app/server deployment lists are in `ml/registry/MODEL_ARTIFACTS.json`, with metrics in `ml/reports/`. Artifacts must carry architecture, category/landmark schema, training manifest hash, registry hash, checkpoint SHA-256, metrics and deployment approval. Torch pickle checkpoints are local trusted artifacts only; service loading must use `weights_only=True`.

Phase 2 spatial v2: two additional randomly initialized, project-authored models actually completed 35 Metal epochs each on the approved aRTF + procedural-v2 corpus. App/server deployment lists still remain empty. Both checkpoints and the selected candidate's ONNX are local ignored research artifacts; their SHA-256, registry and corpus pins, license/attribution obligations and synthetic-only evaluation are recorded in `ml/registry/MODEL_ARTIFACTS.json`, `ml/reports/MODEL_RUNS.json` and `docs/CARDLESS_PHASE_2_STATUS.md`. No third-party pretrained-weight rights are implied. Inference now refuses a changed registry or training manifest instead of silently accepting an old approval snapshot.

Subsequent real-photo acquisition changed the registry identity; the earlier checkpoints consequently fail the current loader gate until reviewed/retrained. Their historical metadata is preserved. `artifacts/secondhand-supervision-smoke/checkpoint.pt` is a new randomly initialized v2 CPU ingestion check with current approval pins: only 2 steps / 8 real classification-only image exposures, no landmark supervision, no useful real-photo performance evaluation and no production approval. This check does not establish centimeter accuracy. Separate app and server deployment arrays remain empty; all flags remain false.
